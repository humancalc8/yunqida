Who we are

A manufacturer, with a team on the ground in Kenya.

Yunqida is a brand of Guangdong Yunqida New Energy Technology Co., Ltd., a high-tech company in Guangzhou, China. It designs household and industrial energy storage, with a focus on solar power systems, lithium batteries and all-in-one energy storage systems.

Our Kenyan branch brings that range to the country, with local advice, quotations and after-sales support.
