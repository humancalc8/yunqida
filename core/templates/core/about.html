Who we are

Global energy technology. Local understanding.

Yunqida is associated with Guangdong Yunqida New Energy Technology Co., Ltd., a company focused on new energy technology and energy storage solutions.

Through Yunqida Energy Technology Kenya, we make a range of solar energy products and battery storage solutions available to Kenyan households, businesses and institutions.

Our focus is to help customers understand their energy requirements, identify suitable equipment and access product guidance and support throughout their projects.
