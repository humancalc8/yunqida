Getting to know Yunqida

More than products.
Powering possibilities.

Better energy decisions begin with understanding your needs and finding technology that makes sense for the way you live and work.

Yunqida Energy Technology is a Kenya-based energy solutions company focused on solar technology, hybrid inverter systems and lithium battery storage.

We bring together products and energy technologies that help homes and businesses explore dependable power options suited to their individual needs.

Whether you are considering a solar installation, looking for backup power or exploring energy storage, our focus is to make the process clearer — from understanding your requirements to exploring available solutions.
