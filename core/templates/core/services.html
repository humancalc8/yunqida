Frequently asked questions

Questions about solar and energy storage?

Find answers to common questions about solar energy systems, lithium batteries, hybrid inverters and energy storage solutions in Kenya.

What solar energy solutions does Yunqida offer?

Yunqida Energy offers access to solar energy products, hybrid inverter systems and lithium battery storage options for residential, commercial and other applications. The appropriate combination depends on your electricity requirements and intended use.

What is a LiFePO4 lithium battery?

LiFePO4 stands for lithium iron phosphate. It is a rechargeable lithium battery chemistry used in energy storage applications. These batteries are available in different voltages, capacities and physical configurations.

What is the difference between a hybrid inverter and a standard inverter?

A hybrid inverter is designed to coordinate multiple energy sources, such as solar panels, batteries and the electricity grid, depending on the model. Other inverter types may have more limited input and energy management capabilities. Product specifications should be reviewed before choosing a system.

Can solar and battery storage be used together?

Yes. Solar panels can generate electricity while compatible battery storage can retain energy for later use. An appropriately configured inverter and battery management system are important parts of the setup.

How do I determine the battery capacity I need?

Battery capacity depends on the appliances and equipment you intend to power, their electricity consumption, how long they need to operate and the usable capacity of the selected battery. System sizing should also account for inverter limitations and installation requirements.

Does Yunqida provide commercial energy storage solutions?

Yunqida provides access to energy storage products and solutions for commercial applications. Businesses can enquire about battery configurations, inverter options and products relevant to their operating requirements.

Can I enquire about OEM and ODM battery products?

Yes. Businesses interested in original equipment manufacturing, product development or private-label energy storage opportunities can contact Yunqida to discuss their requirements and potential product options.

How can I get a quotation for an energy system?

Contact Yunqida with details about your intended application, location, electricity requirements and the products you are considering. This information helps establish which options and quotation details are relevant to your enquiry.
