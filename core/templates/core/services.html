Questions about solar and lithium batteries?

Quick answers on solar systems, LiFePO4 batteries, hybrid inverters and power backups in Kenya.

What is a LiFePO4 lithium battery?
LiFePO4 stands for lithium iron phosphate, a rechargeable battery chemistry widely used for solar storage and power backups, available in many voltages and capacities.
What is the difference between a hybrid inverter and a standard inverter?
A hybrid inverter coordinates solar panels, batteries and the grid, depending on the model, while other inverter types have more limited inputs and energy management.
Can solar and battery storage be used together?
Yes. Solar panels generate electricity and compatible lithium battery storage keeps it for later use, managed by a properly configured inverter.
How do I choose the battery capacity for a power backup?
Capacity depends on the appliances you want to run, their consumption, how long they must operate and the usable capacity of the battery.
How can I get a quotation from Yunqida Energy Kenya?
Contact Yunqida with your application, location, electricity needs and the products you are considering so the team can prepare a relevant quotation.
