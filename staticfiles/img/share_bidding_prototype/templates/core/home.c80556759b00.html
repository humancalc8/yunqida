From bid to confirmed ownership

Four steps, each recorded, so you always know where your shares stand and who holds them.

Step 1

Bid

Place your request while a session is open.

  • Pick a batch from the shares currently available.
  • Choose a maturity class of 3, 7 or 14 days.
  • Submit before the session closes, at 8:00 AM or 8:00 PM EAT.
Step 2

Hold

Your allocation is confirmed and counting down to maturity.

  • See each holding with its reference and principal.
  • Follow the maturity date and the expected value.
  • Check status from your Bought shares page at any time.
Step 3

List

Once eligible, offer your shares to other members.

  • Choose shares to sell once they are eligible.
  • Make them visible to members who want to buy.
  • Wait for a match between buyer and seller.
Step 4

Confirm

The trade is completed and ownership moves to the buyer.

  • Buyer and seller confirm the member-to-member payment.
  • Ownership is recorded against the buyer's account.
  • Review the allocation under Sold shares and Transactions.
